Before booking — short version

Prepare your pet: walk your dog and allow it to relieve itself before the appointment; do not feed your pet immediately before a long session.
Tell us about health, fears, aggression and behaviour in advance.
Mats: 1  / minute; if safe dematting is not possible, the coat will be clipped.
Parasites: extra treatment from 40 .
Owner in the work area only by agreement with the groomer.
Cancel or reschedule in advance. A same-day cancellation or no-show may result in a deposit being required for future bookings.
