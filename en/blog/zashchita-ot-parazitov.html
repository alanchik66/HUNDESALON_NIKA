Protect yourself as well as your four-legged friends from parasites!

28 May 2025

Every responsible owner knows that pets need to be regularly treated for fleas, ticks and worms. However, it is often overlooked that parasites can also pose a threat to humans. Fleas carry helminth eggs, while ticks transmit Lyme disease and encephalitis. Therefore, an anti-parasite programme should include not only treating the pet but also preventive measures for the whole family.

We recommend treating animals year-round, especially if you frequently walk in parks or spend time in nature. At our salon, you can request a safe anti-parasite treatment using professional products. We are also always ready to advise you on choosing products and treatment schedules.

Take care of yourself and your beloved pets!
