Our story

It all started in 2018 with a small 20 m² studio, where I, Nika, picked up professional scissors for the first time. From the very first client I understood: grooming is not just a haircut — it is building trust between a human and an animal. Every pet who came to us taught me something new: to be more patient, attentive, and gentle.

Over five years we grew into a team of three specialists, moved to a spacious salon, but most importantly — preserved the warm atmosphere that our clients love. Today HUNDESALON_NIKA is a place where every pet feels at home.
