About us

HUNDESALON_NIKA is a team of professional groomers united by their love for animals. We care not only about appearance but also about the comfort of every pet. Our specialists continuously improve their skills, use safe products and an individual approach to every breed. The salon is equipped with modern tools, and all procedures take place in a calm, friendly atmosphere.

We accept dogs, cats, rabbits and other small pets. We work by appointment.
